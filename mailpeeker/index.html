Mail Peeker

Stream Deck Plugin · Email at a glance

Mail Peeker is a Stream Deck plugin that displays your recent emails directly on your Stream Deck keys. Connect your email accounts via IMAP and see who's writing to you without switching apps.

Features

  • Real-time email notifications via IMAP IDLE
  • Multiple account support (Gmail, Outlook, any IMAP server)
  • OAuth2 sign-in for Google and Microsoft accounts
  • Password authentication for standard IMAP servers
  • Configurable mailbox and filter options
  • Local-only — no data leaves your machine
